A LITTLE ABOUT US

Learning should open doors for everyone.

Sadachar is an inclusive education platform made to help learners with different abilities access learning with greater confidence and independence.

We bring accessible resources, assistive learning tools and a clear path to support into one welcoming space. Students, parents and volunteers can be part of a community built around dignity, participation and possibility.
